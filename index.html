Why I Listen the Way I Do

Reflections · Music Appreciation

  • Appreciation
  • Worship
  • OPM

I'm not a musician. I don't play an instrument, and I've never written a song. What I do have is a deep appreciation for how music makes me feel, and how a good song can carry a message better than a paragraph ever could.

For me, listening to music is closer to how I read scripture — slowly, more than once, paying attention to the words as much as the melody. A worship song from Hillsong or Elevation hits differently when I actually sit with the lyrics instead of letting it play in the background.

On the OPM side, bands like Eraserheads and Parokya Ni Edgar remind me of growing up, while December Avenue and Ben&Ben capture the quieter, more reflective moods I need after a long week of school.

  • Worship songs during quiet time and scripture reading.
